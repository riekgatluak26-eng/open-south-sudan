How we work

Community-led, evidence-driven, open by default.

Four steps. One promise: nothing we build stays on paper — it reaches the people who need it most.

  1. 1

    Step 01

    Listen

    We start in the community — mapping the problem with the people who live it, in their language and on their terms.

    Field visits Co-design
  2. 2

    Step 02

    Build

    We prototype lightweight tools that work on low bandwidth, basic devices and across multiple South Sudanese languages.

    Low-bandwidth Offline-first
  3. 3

    Step 03

    Test

    We pilot with local partners, measure what actually changes and iterate fast until the numbers — and the people — say it works.

    Pilots Impact data
  4. 4

    Step 04

    Scale & share

    We open-source the code, publish the data and hand ownership to communities — so the solution outlives us.

    Open source Open data

Our promise: no pilot without a plan to hand it over.

Every project ships with documentation, training and a local owner.
